LeadOps helps businesses import their leads and follow up with them over WhatsApp and email. This policy covers two groups: account holders (businesses and individuals who sign up) and leads (people whose details a customer has uploaded and who may receive messages through LeadOps).

1. Who is responsible for which data

For account holder data, we are the controller. For lead data that a customer uploads, the customer is the controller and we act as a processor, handling the data only on the customer's instructions. If you are a lead, contact the business that messaged you first. You can also contact us and we will pass your request on or act on it where we can.

2. Data we collect

From account holders:

  • Name, email address, company name, and login credentials.
  • Saved settings such as message templates, sequence timing, and sender details.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, and usage logs.

About leads, uploaded by customers:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and company from the uploaded CSV file.
  • Any extra columns the customer chooses to include.

Generated while the service runs:

  • A log of each message sent, with its channel (WhatsApp or email), time, and delivery status.
  • Replies. When a lead replies to an email or messages the sender's WhatsApp number, we record the reply, its time, and the content needed to show the conversation and set the lead's status to Opportunity.
  • Opt-out requests.

3. How we use data

  • To import leads and send scheduled WhatsApp and email messages for the customer.
  • To detect replies, stop the sequence for that lead, and mark the lead as an Opportunity.
  • To show delivery and pipeline status in the dashboard.
  • To secure, maintain, debug, and improve the service.
  • To contact account holders about their account, billing, and important changes.
  • To meet legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use lead data for our own marketing.

4. Service providers and third parties

We share data with these providers only as needed to send and track messages:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform, Cloud API): delivers WhatsApp template messages and reports delivery status and incoming replies to us. Meta's policies apply to data it processes: whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Zoho Mail: sends and receives email. See zoho.com/privacy.html.
  • Hosting and database providers: [HOSTING PROVIDER], [DATABASE PROVIDER].

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, to protect rights and safety, or in a merger or acquisition, with notice to affected customers.

5. Consent and opt-out

Customers must have a lawful basis and, where the law or WhatsApp's policies require it, a valid opt-in before uploading a lead and messaging them. Customers are responsible for this.

Every email includes an unsubscribe option. A lead can reply STOP to a WhatsApp message to opt out. After an opt-out, the lead's status is Unsubscribed and no further messages are sent.

6. Legal bases

Where laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on contract (providing the service to account holders), legitimate interests (security, service improvement, and reaching business contacts), consent (for example WhatsApp opt-in), and legal obligation. Where India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 applies, we process data on the basis of consent or other uses that Act permits.

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, protected storage for API tokens, and webhook signature verification. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify affected customers and authorities as the law requires.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than where you live. Where required, we use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.
