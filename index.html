LeadOps Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026 Operated by: [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS] Contact: [PRIVACY EMAIL]

LeadOps is a tool that helps businesses import their leads and follow up with them over WhatsApp and email. This policy explains what personal data LeadOps handles, why, and what choices you have. It covers two groups of people:

  • Account holders: businesses and individuals who sign up for LeadOps ("you" or "customers").
  • Leads: people whose details a customer has uploaded to LeadOps and who may receive messages sent through it ("recipients").

1. Who is responsible for which data

For data about account holders, we are the controller. For lead data that a customer uploads, the customer is the controller and we act as a processor, handling the data only on the customer's instructions. If you are a lead and want to exercise your rights, contact the business that messaged you first. You can also contact us and we will pass your request on or act on it where we can.

2. Data we collect

From account holders

  • Name, email address, company name, and login credentials.
  • Settings you save, such as message templates, sequence timing, and connected sender details.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, and usage logs.

About leads (uploaded by customers)

  • Name, email address, phone number, and company, as provided in the uploaded CSV file.
  • Any additional columns the customer chooses to include.

Generated while the service runs

  • A log of each message sent, its channel (WhatsApp or email), time, and delivery status.
  • Replies: when a lead replies to an email or sends a WhatsApp message to the sender's number, we record that a reply occurred, its time, and its content as needed to show the conversation and update the lead's status to "Opportunity".
  • Opt-out requests (unsubscribes).

3. How we use data

  • To import leads and send scheduled WhatsApp and email messages on the customer's behalf.
  • To detect replies, stop the automated sequence for that lead, and mark the lead as an opportunity.
  • To show delivery and pipeline status in the dashboard.
  • To secure, maintain, debug, and improve the service.
  • To contact account holders about their account, billing, and important changes.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use lead data for our own marketing.

4. Service providers and third parties

LeadOps relies on the following providers to deliver messages. Data is shared with them only as needed to send and track messages.

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform / Cloud API): delivers WhatsApp template messages and reports delivery status and incoming replies to us. Meta's own policies apply to data it processes: https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Zoho Mail: sends and receives email. See https://www.zoho.com/privacy.html
  • Hosting and database providers: [HOSTING PROVIDER], [DATABASE PROVIDER].

We may also disclose data if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in a merger or acquisition (with notice to affected customers).

5. Consent and opt-out

Customers must have a lawful basis, and where required by law or by WhatsApp's policies, a valid opt-in, before uploading a lead and messaging them. Customers are responsible for this.

Every email includes an unsubscribe option, and a lead can reply "STOP" to a WhatsApp message to opt out. When a lead opts out, their status is set to "Unsubscribed" and no further messages are sent.

6. Legal bases

Where laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on: performance of a contract (providing the service to account holders), legitimate interests (security, service improvement, and, for customers, reaching business contacts), consent (where required, for example WhatsApp opt-in), and legal obligation. Where India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 applies, we process data on the basis of consent or other permitted legitimate uses under that Act.

  • Account data is kept while the account is active and for [X] days after closure.
  • Lead data and message logs are kept for as long as the customer keeps them in LeadOps. Customers can delete leads at any time, and we delete remaining lead data within [X] days after account closure.

8. Security

We use measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), access controls, secret management for API tokens, and webhook signature verification. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify affected customers and authorities as required by law.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than where you live. Where required, we use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. To make a request, email [PRIVACY EMAIL]. We will respond within the time required by applicable law, and we may need to verify your identity first.

11. Children

LeadOps is a business tool and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child's data has been uploaded, contact us and we will remove it.

12. Cookies
